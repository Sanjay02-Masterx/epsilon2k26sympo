About the Event

EPSILON 2K26

Welcome to Epsilon 2K26 — the annual national-level technical symposium of Meenakshi Sundararajan Engineering College. A battleground for the brightest minds, where innovation meets passion.

With a legacy of excellence, Epsilon brings together students from colleges across the nation to compete, collaborate, and celebrate the spirit of engineering. From cutting-edge technical challenges to creative non-technical events — there's something for everyone.

Featuring exciting cash prizes, workshops by industry experts, and an electrifying atmosphere — Epsilon 2K26 is the event of the year you don't want to miss.
